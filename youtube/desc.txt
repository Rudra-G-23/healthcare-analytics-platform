This video is part of my Healthcare Analytics Platform project, where I am transforming a healthcare datasets into an industry-style analytics solution.

The goal of this project is to analyze hospital operations, emergency care performance, patient flow, resource utilization, and operational efficiency to uncover actionable insights that support better decision-making.

In this series, I document the complete journey—from raw data and real-world errors to debugging, problem-solving, data modeling, analytics, and dashboard development. Rather than showing only polished results, I share the actual development process, challenges encountered, and how they are resolved.
